# 🛍️ Retail Customer Behaviour Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing retail customer behavior to uncover meaningful insights about purchasing patterns, customer segmentation, and sales trends. The goal is to transform raw transactional data into actionable business insights.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Understand customer purchasing behavior
* Identify high-value and repeat customers
* Analyze sales trends across different time periods
* Discover factors influencing customer spending

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Python** (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn)
* **SQL** (Data querying and analysis)
* **Power BI / Tableau** (Interactive dashboard and visualization)

---

## 📊 Key Insights

* A significant portion of revenue is generated by repeat customers
* Certain customer segments contribute more to overall sales
* Sales show noticeable variation across different days and periods
* Discount strategies influence customer purchase frequency

---

## 💼 Business Recommendations

* Implement customer retention strategies to increase repeat purchases
* Target high-value customers with personalized marketing campaigns
* Optimize discount and promotional strategies
* Improve inventory planning based on sales trends

---


---

## 📈 Outcome

This project demonstrates how data analysis techniques can be used to extract valuable insights from retail data and support data-driven decision-making.
